PLACE YOUR DREAMCAST GAMES HERE (legal copies).

Supported formats:
  .chd  — GD-ROM image (recommended, single file)
  .zip  — ZIP containing a .gdi + its tracks (track01.bin, etc.)
  .cue  — plus the .bin files of its tracks in the same folder
  .gdi  — plus its tracks in the same folder

The emulator detects the format automatically and adds the game to the menu.
No editing needed.

Dreamcast games are copyrighted by their respective owners. You must provide
your own legally obtained copies. This package does not include any games.
