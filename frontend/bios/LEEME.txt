PLACE YOUR DREAMCAST BIOS HERE (legal, from your own console).

Required files:
  dc_boot.bin   (main BIOS, 2 MB)
  dc_flash.bin  (region flash, 128 KB)

Optional:
  dc_nvmem.bin  (non-volatile memory)

The emulator finds them automatically. If they are missing, the page will
show a "BIOS missing" warning.

The Dreamcast BIOS is copyrighted by Sega. You must provide your own legally
obtained copy dumped from hardware you own. This package does not include it.
